Probably Human Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Introduction

Probably Human is a game for iOS and Android made by Swaynt LLC ("we," "our," or "us"). This policy explains what the game collects, why, and what you can do about it. Our general Privacy Policy covers our website; for the game, this page applies.

In short: there is no account, your game stays on your device, the game shows no ads and does not track you across other apps, and the anonymous usage data it shares can be switched off in the game.

No Account, Progress on Your Device

The game has no sign-in. Your file (answers, results, settings) is saved only on your device. We never receive it. Deleting the app, or burning the file in the game, deletes it.

Anonymous Usage Data

To learn how the game is played and where it can be better, the game sends anonymous usage data through Google Analytics for Firebase:

  • Gameplay events: a session started or ended, how many items it lasted, how it ended, awards earned, the first card turned over, the Subject file opened
  • Purchase steps: the purchase screen shown, a purchase started, completed, cancelled or restored, and the price shown (never payment details)
  • Whether you were shown or opened the research invite (below)
  • A random app-instance identifier created on install, plus device model, operating system and app version, language, and approximate region (country or city, derived from your IP address, which is not stored)

This data is not linked to your name, email or any account, contains nothing you write, and is not used for advertising or tracking. The game does not use the advertising identifier on either platform and does not ask for permission to track you.

Turning it off: open the Subject file, go to Record, and untick "Share anonymous usage data". The game then sends no further usage data. Usage data is kept for no longer than 14 months.

Game Settings From Our Server

At launch the game fetches a few settings from Firebase Remote Config (for example, how many sessions are free, and whether a research study is running). This uses a Firebase installation identifier and sends no gameplay data.

Purchases

The full version is a one-time purchase handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details, name or store account. The store tells the game only whether the full version is owned, and the game remembers that on your device. Offer and promo codes are redeemed in the store's own screens.

User Research

Sometimes the game invites you to an optional survey about the game. It only appears while a study is running, and opening it is your choice. The survey opens in your browser on an external website, whose own privacy policy applies to anything you enter there.

If anonymous usage data is on, the survey link carries the game's anonymous app-instance identifier, so we can relate answers to anonymous gameplay data (for example, how many sessions were played). With usage data off, the link carries no identifier.

What We Don't Do

  • No ads and no advertising identifiers
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No selling or sharing of data for advertising
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone

Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Remote Config — anonymous usage data and game settings. Firebase Privacy
  • Apple App Store — purchases on iOS. Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Play — purchases on Android. Google Privacy Policy

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. Because the usage data is anonymous and not linked to you, we usually cannot find a particular person's data. You can stop collection at any time with the switch above, and uninstalling the app resets its identifier. For any request or question, contact us at .

Children's Privacy

Probably Human is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the game changes. We will post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date.
